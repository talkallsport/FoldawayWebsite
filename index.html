2. Adding New Photos or GIFs to the Hero Auto-Scroller

Scroll to const SHOWCASE_SLIDES = [ at the bottom of index.html and paste a new slide block in the order you want it to scroll: 

{
  group: "Match Animations & GIFs", // Options: "Live Scoreboards", "Match Animations & GIFs", "COB & Installations"
  title: "Boundary Four Flame Animation",
  file: "media/BoundaryFour.gif",
  description: "Full-screen animated boundary graphic triggered on score update."
},
